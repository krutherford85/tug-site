# Tug Support

Need help with Tug, found a problem, or have an idea? Email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We read every message.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iPhone model, the iOS version (Settings > General > About) and what you were doing when it happened.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone. Tug has no accounts and no servers, so we never see your information. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**Is my data backed up?**
Tug's data is included in your regular iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). Tug doesn't sync between devices yet.

**How do I turn reminders on or off?**
Go to Settings > Notifications > Tug. Tug reminds you to follow up a week after applying if nothing has changed, and the day before and one hour before each scheduled interview.

**Why didn't filling in from a link work?**
Some job sites require signing in or block automatic reading of their pages. When that happens, Tug tells you and keeps the link with the application, so you can fill in the details yourself.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete individual applications and contacts in the app, or delete Tug from your iPhone to remove all of its data. Copies may remain in your own backups until they're replaced.
