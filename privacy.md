title: Tug Privacy Policy
---

**Effective November 22, 2026**

Tug is a job search organizer for iPhone, made by Kelly Rutherford (Pruned) ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Tug.

**The short version:** your job search stays on your iPhone. We don't collect, see, store, sell or share any of your information. Tug has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What Tug stores, and where

Everything you enter in Tug stays on your iPhone. That includes applications, companies, interviews, contacts, salaries and notes. We have no servers, so this information is never sent to us.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Tug's data is included in that backup like any other app's data. Apple's privacy policy covers those backups.

## Filling in an application from a link

If you paste a link to a job posting, Tug downloads that page so it can fill in the details for you. The request goes straight from your iPhone to the job site, the same as opening the link in Safari. For Greenhouse links, Tug uses Greenhouse's public job board API.

Like any website you visit, the job site receives the request and standard connection information such as your IP address. Its own privacy policy applies to that. Tug sends nothing else, and nothing passes through us. If you don't paste a link, Tug makes no network connections.

## Notifications

Tug can remind you to follow up on applications and before interviews. These reminders are scheduled and delivered on your iPhone. They aren't push notifications from a server. You can turn them off at any time in Settings > Notifications > Tug.

## Calendar and Contacts

Tug can add an interview to Calendar or a person to Contacts, but only through Apple's standard screens, where you review and confirm the entry. Tug never asks for access to your calendar or contacts, and never reads them.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect personal information through the app.
- We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, or any third-party code that collects data.
- We don't sell or share information. We have nothing to share.

## If you email us

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems you report, and we don't add you to any mailing list or share it with anyone. Our email is hosted by Apple's iCloud Mail. To have your messages deleted, just ask.

## Your control over your data

Because your data only lives on your iPhone, you're always in control of it. You can edit or delete anything in the app. Deleting Tug deletes all of its data from your iPhone, although copies may remain in your own backups until those are replaced.

## Children

Tug is meant for adults looking for work and isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a future version of Tug ever collects information, we'll update this policy before that version is released and explain the change in the app.
